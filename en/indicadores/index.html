Indicators

The 34 municipal indicators of the RESA-MT Observatory, grouped into six themes, with a map of the 141 municipalities of Mato Grosso, rankings within each biome and across the state, and a table showing the IBGE symbols
Preliminary version for the project team. Results may change as the study progresses. English translation under review. If the two versions differ, the Portuguese text prevails.

The observatory covers 34 indicators, grouped into six themes, for the 141 municipalities of Mato Grosso (MT). Each indicator page has:

  • a choropleth map of the 141 municipalities, with classes based on quintiles and X shown in grey;
  • a ranking within each biome and across Mato Grosso, highlighting the three pilot-area host municipalities;
  • a time series, where available, for indicators from the Greenhouse Gas Emissions and Removals Estimation System (SEEG);
  • aggregates by biome and by Manuscript 1 (M1) type;
  • the full table, with a column for the symbols used by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) and an option to download the filtered rows.

Each card shows the value for Mato Grosso: the municipal sum for totals, the ratio of sums for proportions and means, and the change in sums for dynamics indicators. Key indicators carry the key badge.

IBGE standard symbols: X suppressed (value withheld to avoid identifying the respondent) · – true zero · ... not available. None of these symbols is shown as zero.

Tenure and agrarian reform

This theme covers the producer’s land tenure status (IBGE, 2017 Agricultural Census) and the settlement projects of the National Institute for Colonisation and Agrarian Reform (INCRA). The projects are recorded in the Agrarian Reform Projects Information System (SIPRA, Report 0227, as at 3 June 2025). Category 46504 understates the extent of agrarian reform, and SIPRA counts each project only in its host municipality. Indicators in this theme: 7.

Climate (SEEG 13)

This theme covers greenhouse gas emissions and removals estimated by SEEG 13 (GWP-AR5) for the whole municipality, not only for family farming. The series run from 1970 to 2024 for gross emissions and for agriculture and livestock emissions, and from 1990 to 2024 for land-use change and removals. Indicators in this theme: 4.
